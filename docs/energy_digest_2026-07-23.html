0.95 Relevance
Junhong Liu, Qinfei Long, Alex Pengfei Zhao et al. · Jul 21, 2026

Quantum-resistant distributed optimization is necessary for multi-region unit commitment due to vulnerable encrypted data flow against retrospective decryption.
