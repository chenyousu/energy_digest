A subspace approach to data-driven predictive control for linear parameter-varying systems
0.70 Relevance
Federico Porcari, Chris Verhoek, Valentina Breschi et al. · Jul 30, 2026

A subspace approach is presented for data-driven predictive control of linear parameter-varying systems, using a multi-step predictor that separates past data, future inputs, scheduling trajectories, and innovations. The proposed method projects this representation onto lifted input-output-scheduling data to obtain an asymptotically unbiased data-driven predictor, which can be embedded in a receding-horizon control problem without explicitly identifying an LPV model. This approach achieves better robustness to measurement noise and reduced computational cost compared to existing schemes.

Why This Matters
This paper's focus on predictive control for linear parameter-varying systems, which can be applied to power system modeling and operation, is relevant to grid operators and utility planners. The proposed data-driven approach could improve robustness to measurement noise and enhance tracking performance in power system operations, making it a useful contribution for enhancing resilience and reliability in grid management.
