Battery storage capacity averaged 70% growth over the last three years

Storage
Source: EIA Today in Energy
Published: August 07, 2026

Summary

Battery storage capacity in the United States averaged a 70% annual growth rate over the last three years. The U.S. power system had operational battery storage capacity of 43.6 gigawatts (GW) by the end of 2025, with an additional 8.3 GW added in the first six months of 2026. By mid-2026, total nameplate battery storage capacity reached nearly 52 GW.
