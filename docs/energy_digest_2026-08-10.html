Solar

Summary

France expands renewable curtailment mechanism amid negative prices, lowers threshold to 1 MW

Solar
Source: PV Magazine
Published: August 10, 2026

Summary

France is expanding its renewable curtailment mechanism to apply to installations with a capacity as low as 1 MW, down from the previous threshold of 10 MW. The change aims to align renewable power generation with periods of electricity oversupply when market prices turn negative. The new rules will be implemented in two stages, starting on December 1, 2026, and will apply to most types of photovoltaic and wind installations benefiting from certain forms of public support.
