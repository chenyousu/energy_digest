The U.S. Interconnection Queue System: Cascading Vulnerability Analysis and a Resilience Engineering Framework
0.90 Relevance
Zahra Heidari · Sep 09, 2026

The US interconnection queues contain approximately 2,061 GW of capacity and have only 13% of queued projects operational since 2000-2020. The system is vulnerable to self-reinforcing project withdrawal cascades due to restudy and cost reallocation, which can amplify by up to 8.7x under certain conditions. Capping per-neighbor cost reallocation reduces cascade magnitude, highlighting the need for a more robust design in the interconnection queue's cost allocation mechanism.

Why This Matters
This paper is highly relevant to power system engineers as it presents a resilience engineering framework for analyzing the U.S. interconnection queue system, which directly affects grid operations and capacity markets.
