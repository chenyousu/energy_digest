Large-Scale Partition-Based RIS Beamforming For Uplink RIS-Equipped Multi-User Systems: Asymptotic Analysis
0.80 Relevance
Samira Rahimian, Haris Gacanin · Oct 08, 2026

A K-user uplink system with a large-scale RIS preceding ZF reception at the base station shows that the orthogonal projector underlying the ZF SINR converges to a rank-one matrix aligned with the desired user's channel. This convergence allows for a closed-form asymptotic approximation of the average per-user SINR, which is approximately sum-rate-optimal regardless of path-loss asymmetry across users. A low-complexity greedy pairwise-transfer search refines the partition to achieve modest sum-rate gains.

Why This Matters
This paper's focus on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS) and beamforming for multi-user systems has significant implications for the integration of RIS technology in future wireless networks, which could lead to improved performance and efficiency in various applications such as 5G and 6G communication systems relevant to power industry professionals. The potential benefits of this technology can be leveraged in smart grid operations, energy management, and renewable energy integration, making it a relevant area for the power industry.
