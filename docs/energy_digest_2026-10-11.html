Hydrogen production from seawater via integrated desalination and electrolysis at DICP

Hydrogen
Source: Hydrogen Fuel News
Published: October 11, 2026

Summary

Researchers at Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics have reported an integrated seawater-to-hydrogen-and-fresh-water process called the STHW process, which combines alkaline water electrolysis with low-temperature vacuum-distillation desalination to produce high-purity hydrogen and distilled freshwater. The system uses low-grade waste heat generated by the electrolyzer to drive seawater evaporation under reduced pressure, avoiding challenges related to corrosion and scaling. This approach improves operational stability by separating the desalination step from direct electrolysis of untreated seawater.
