This Hidden Device Could Free Up Gigawatts of Grid Power

Grid
Source: IEEE Spectrum Energy
Published: October 17, 2026

Summary

Companies can install VFDs to optimize motor performance, with only 16% of industrial facilities and 4% of commercial-sector motors currently using this technology. Dutch regulator flags EU cyber gap for cloud-controlled home batteries

Grid
Source: PV Magazine
Published: October 17, 2026

Summary

The Dutch regulator warns of a cyber gap in EU cloud-controlled home batteries, citing the risk of a single point of entry controlling many devices, which could destabilize the grid with a sudden imbalance of 3 GW or more. The regulator argues that existing EU regulations, such as the Cyber Resilience Act and NIS2 directive, only partly cover companies behind platform backend systems and cloud platforms. A faulty update or cyberattack on these systems could have severe consequences, particularly with the rapid growth of heat pumps and EV chargers in the Netherlands.
