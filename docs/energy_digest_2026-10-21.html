Latency-Neutral Passive Cyber Monitoring for Grid-Edge Operational Technology Networks
0.80 Relevance
Damian Byrne, Emad Abukhousa, Jason Reed et al. · Oct 20, 2026

The Operational Technology Supervisor (OTSV) is a three-tier passive monitoring framework designed to capture security telemetry in deterministic industrial networks without degrading timing-sensitive grid-edge infrastructure. The framework utilizes a Layer-3-unconfigured passive ingestion mode, isolating the deep packet inspection engine from the production network, and achieves stable latency distributions with a latency delta of less than 0.12 milliseconds. This approach enables continuous high-fidelity security observation alongside stringent metering timing bounds.

Why This Matters
This paper matters for power system engineers as it presents a novel solution for monitoring grid-edge operational technology networks, which is critical for ensuring the reliability and security of the grid. By mitigating latency issues, this framework enables continuous high-fidelity security observation, directly supporting the compliance with regulatory standards such as NERC CIP and FERC requirements.
