Power-Swing Blocking Scheme Based on Continuous and Higher-Order Incremental Quantities for Systems with Inverter-Based Resources
0.90 Relevance
Henrik Johansson, Qianli Xing, Nathaniel Taylor et al. · Nov 05, 2026

A new power-swing blocking scheme uses higher-order incremental quantities to improve performance in systems with inverter-based resources, addressing interoperability issues with faster power swings and unconventional impedance trajectories. The proposed scheme outperforms conventional dual-blinder methods in simulations, particularly in challenging swing scenarios. The scheme is designed to handle the limitations of traditional blinders, such as not operating when the measured impedance is within the blinders.

Why This Matters
This paper matters for power system engineers as it addresses the challenges of inverter-based resources on power-swing blocking schemes, which is crucial for ensuring reliable distance protection and maintaining grid stability, especially with increasing penetration levels of renewable energy sources. The proposed scheme has practical implications for grid operators and utility planners seeking to optimize their distance relay settings for systems with inverter-based resources.

0.80 Relevance
Matthew Van Ham, Yiping Liu, Xiaozhe Wang · Nov 05, 2026

EnergyBench: a live, gate-aligned benchmark of day-ahead forecasts for European power markets
0.80 Relevance
Evgenii Genov, Ander Martinez Alonso, Christoph Bergmeir · Nov 05, 2026

EnergyBench is a live, gate-aligned benchmark for day-ahead forecasts of European power markets, evaluating 18 time-series foundation models and their performance without and with weather covariates. The benchmark shows that including weather covariates reduces the error of each model by 37-51% on matched races, with some models outperforming a LightGBM model. The grid operators' pre-gate forecasts remain ahead in wind and solar forecasts, but the models with weather covariates close the gap in load forecasts.

Why This Matters
This paper matters for power system engineers as it provides a live, gate-aligned benchmark for day-ahead forecasts, enabling more accurate predictions of energy demand and supply. This, in turn, can inform grid operators' decisions on capacity allocation and resource management, ultimately improving the resilience and efficiency of power systems.
