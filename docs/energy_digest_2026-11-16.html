Summary

Solar, wind and batteries can deliver firm power at $0.086/kWh, says Ember

Solar
Source: PV Magazine
Published: November 16, 2026

Summary

Adding wind power to hybrid systems combining photovoltaics and battery storage can reduce the cost of meeting 90% of electricity demand by nearly one-fifth, with the median cost falling from $0.105/kWh to $0.086/kWh. The inclusion of wind power reduces storage requirements, primarily in regions like Canada, the United States, and Europe, where battery storage capacity declines from 20-24 hours to 10-12 hours. With wind power included, three-quarters of global electricity demand can be met at 90% coverage for less than $0.10/kWh.

Dutch watchdog urges solar owners to check energy contracts

Solar
Source: PV Magazine
Published: November 16, 2026

Summary

The Netherlands Authority for Consumers and Markets (ACM) is advising households with solar installations to check their energy contracts for suitability ahead of the end of net-metering in 2027, as they will no longer be able to offset exported electricity against imported electricity. From 2027, households will pay the full retail electricity rate for power drawn from the grid, and receive a separate feed-in payment for surplus electricity. The regulator expects feed-in charges to decline after the scheme ends, but notes that suppliers will still face costs related to balancing the grid with solar generation.
