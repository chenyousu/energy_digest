0.80 Relevance
Amber He Wei, Shiyu Cheng, Andrew Clark et al. · Nov 16, 2026

A decentralized event-triggered topology-switching protocol has been developed for multi-agent systems, allowing agents to disconnect while preserving state safety and excluding Zeno behavior. The protocol uses locally computable certificates to determine when to connect and disconnect from the network, improving scalability and reducing computational complexity. The protocol ensures estimation error remains within a prescribed threshold and is non-Zeno, reducing the time agents remain connected while maintaining safe operating conditions.

0.80 Relevance
Tom Kaufmann, Johann Reger · Nov 16, 2026

Concurrent learning guarantees precise identification of unknown parameters when data with finite excitation is collected online. 0.80 Relevance
Hongyu Wu, Yichen Liu, Bo Liu · Nov 16, 2026

Quantum-assisted D-FACTS scheduling improves moving target defense by allocating limited physical checks through candidate sharing and schedule-completion evaluation. TRACK: Tool-Augmented Reasoning over Time Series for Fault Diagnosis in Power Management System
0.80 Relevance
Erkan Bayram, Debanjan Ghosh, Li Zhu et al. · Nov 16, 2026

Track, a tool-augmented reasoning system, uses a time series encoder and small language model to produce structured diagnostic reports for fault diagnosis in power management systems. The system achieves over 95% fault-category accuracy on a 15-class multi-load simulated benchmark and generalizes to out-of-distribution faults. It operates on-device, keeping proprietary sensor data local, and surpasses the best encoder-probing baseline by 37% accuracy.

The proposed TRACK tool can help grid operators and utility planners to quickly and accurately diagnose faults, enabling swift decision-making and reducing downtime, ultimately leading to improved grid operations and reliability.

Optimal Network Reduction for Distribution Systems extends Opti-KRON to include uncertain nodal injections, using robust uncertainty sets and a Wasserstein ambiguity set to achieve voltage magnitude error guarantees.
