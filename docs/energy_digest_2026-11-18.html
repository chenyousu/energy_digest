Safely Mitigating Grid Imbalance: A Hybrid Distributional Wide-Margin Recourse Approach
0.80 Relevance
Shoaib Hussain, Mostafa Farrokhabadi, Hamidreza Zareipour · Nov 17, 2026

The MADWMPG framework proposes a dual-policy hybrid imitation and safe reinforcement learning method to safely mitigate grid imbalance in active distribution networks. The framework uses a wide-margin constraint barrier recourse module to learn proactively suppress exploratory violations, addressing operational volatility with a novel adaptation of noncrossing quantile regression distributional critics. The framework was trained and tested on a modified multiphase IEEE 123-bus test feeder, demonstrating superiority over conventional reinforcement learning and SRL methods.

Why This Matters
This paper is highly relevant to power system engineers as it proposes a novel approach to safely mitigating grid imbalance, a critical issue in modern power grids, particularly with increasing renewable energy integration. The proposed framework can help grid operators and planners develop more effective strategies for managing grid volatility and ensuring grid stability, making it a valuable contribution to the field of grid operations and resilience.
