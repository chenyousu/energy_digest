0.70 Relevance
Aihui Liu, Magnus Jansson · Nov 19, 2026

Model predictive control (MPC) applies the first input of an optimized sequence without using all available data, and this decision is influenced by model uncertainty, with actions agreeing and disagreeing across models. The framework measures the mean-squared deviation of actions from the fitted model's input, with model uncertainty affecting predictions and actions differently. Model uncertainty has a distinct impact on the first action applied by MPC.

Thermal-Guided Ensemble Reinforcement Learning for Regime-Adaptive Short-Term GPU Power Forecasting in AI Data Centers
0.80 Relevance
Yuanshuo Zhang, Ying Zhang, Ziying Wang et al. · Nov 19, 2026

A thermal-guided ensemble reinforcement learning framework is proposed for probabilistic load forecasting in AI data centers, which reduces the normalized mean absolute error to about 1% compared to existing benchmarks. The framework incorporates a thermal-guided neural correction to estimate the ensemble residual and a sensitivity-projection regularizer to enforce non-negative sensitivity of power to GPU utilization and temperature. The method converges faster with narrower prediction intervals than a DRL ensemble without thermal guidance.

Why This Matters
The paper's focus on thermal-guided ensemble reinforcement learning for regime-adaptive short-term GPU power forecasting in AI data centers has significant practical implications for grid operators and utility planners, as it can help optimize energy management and predictive grid control in the face of bursty and regime-dependent load patterns. This can be particularly relevant in the context of ISO operations, capacity markets, and utility planning, where accurate predictive forecasting is critical for ensuring grid stability and reliability.
