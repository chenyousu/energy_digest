Hydrogen shifts summer solar to winter in Japanese apartment building

Solar
Source: PV Magazine
Published: November 23, 2026

Summary

A Japanese apartment building's hydrogen-linked energy management system successfully shifted surplus summer solar power into winter, meeting 95% of electricity demand from on-site renewable energy. The system, installed in April 2025, uses surplus solar electricity to produce green hydrogen, which is stored and converted back into electricity and heat in winter. The project, developed by Takenaka Corp., marks the first installation in Japan of a hydrogen-utilizing energy management system in an apartment building.

Europe turns negative electricity prices into a flexibility signal

Storage
Source: PV Magazine
Published: November 23, 2026

Summary

Negative electricity prices in European power markets do not necessarily indicate a failure, but rather can be used to direct investment towards energy storage, demand-side flexibility, and electrification when they accurately reflect supply and demand conditions. The frequency and duration of negative prices vary across markets, influenced by factors such as renewable penetration, storage deployment, and grid constraints. Spain and the Netherlands recorded negative prices during approximately 7% of the hours in 2025, highlighting the need for careful analysis to distinguish between economic signals and regulatory distortions.
