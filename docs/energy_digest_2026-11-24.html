Solar
Source: PV Magazine
Published: November 24, 2026

Summary

The country's installed solar capacity is 1,040 MW, but its electricity demand is only 650 MW, leading to solar curtailment of up to 47% in 2025. The government is implementing energy storage, electricity interconnection, and market reforms to address the imbalance.
